How to read this

Hours per day are averaged over all seven days. A 40-hour-a-week position shows as 5.7 hours a day (40 ÷ 7). One 8-hour shift every day of the week is 8 hours a day and 1.4 FTE. FTE = hours per day × 7 ÷ 40. "Today" is worked hours from the punch clock, July 5 to August 29, 2026, so PTO is excluded and salaried managers who punch short days look smaller than the salary they are paid (a salaried director who punches 26 hours a week and takes leave in the window shows as 3.7 hours a day; the payroll still pays 40). Dollar figures use January 2027 wage rates with a 19% benefit load, and agency at its invoice rate.
